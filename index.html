 Helpful to know
Questions before you book?


Can children book online consultations?
Yes, for non-urgent pediatric concerns with a parent or guardian participating.


Are medical certificates guaranteed?
No. Medical certificates, fit-to-work or fit-to-travel assessments, prescriptions and laboratory requests depend on the physician's assessment and applicable requirements.


Are tube insertion, catheters and wound care done online?
No. These require an appropriate in-person assessment and scheduled visit, subject to clinical suitability, equipment and availability.


How do I pay?
Once the schedule and professional fee are confirmed, payment may be arranged through GCash, Maya or bank transfer. Do not pay before confirming directly with the clinic.


Does completing this form confirm my appointment?
No. The form only creates a message on your device. Copy and send it using Dr. Raeselle's Facebook page, then wait for confirmation.




For emergencies, seek immediate in-person care.
This online service is not for severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, major bleeding, seizures, unconsciousness, or other urgent medical conditions.
